---
name: pr-draft-summary
description: Prepare a concise research-grade pull request handoff for AdaLiFT changes.
---

Use when substantial work is ready for review.

Produce:
- What changed and why.
- Whether behavior is paper-faithful, infrastructure-only, or an AdaLiFT extension.
- Key design decisions and compatibility impact.
- Tests/verification run and results.
- Experiment evidence, if any, with evidence level.
- Known limitations or follow-up work.
- Reviewer focus areas.

Do not inflate smoke tests into research claims.
