# Code of Conduct

We want AdaLiFT to be a rigorous and welcoming research community.

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-driven. Critique ideas and results, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Scientific disagreement is expected; present reproducible evidence and clearly distinguish observation from interpretation.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to Ahmed Jawed at `ahmed.jawed32@gmail.com`, including relevant links and context. Reports are handled discreetly; information is shared only as needed to investigate and respond. If a report concerns the current sole maintainer, this project does not yet have an independent appeal body.
